\title{A compact ILP for exact $|C|$\\
over the full differential-link universe}
\author{}
\date{}

\begin{document}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
\noindent
\texttt{ilp\_baseline} solves exact $|C|$ by emitting one binary per
valid link. The full universe of those links --- every clash-free
sub-run of every maximal source run, as enumerated by
\texttt{enumerate\_all\_links\_} --- has $\Theta(L^2)$ members per run of
length $L$, so the expanded CPLEX model is unusable on tandem inputs
(a $200\times 50$ repeat under $k{=}21$, $a_{\max}{=}8$ has $m=10001$
ranks and about $7.7$ million sub-runs). Every such sub-run is an
interval of a maximal run, so it is enough to decide, per run, a single
consecutive occupancy mask. The resulting compact ILP has
$O(m\,a_{\max})$ binaries, is equivalent to the expanded model, and is
what one should solve when the expanded LP will not even write.
\end{abstract}

\section{The expanded set-packing ILP}
\label{sec:expanded}

We use the notation of \texttt{tree\_dp3}: a valid link
$k = (c_k, a_k, S_k, T_k)$ has target name $c_k$, shift $a_k$, contiguous
source ranks $S_k = [s_{\mathrm{lo}}, s_{\mathrm{hi}})$ and covered ranks
$T_k$, with $\orig(T_k[j]) = \orig(S_k[j]) + a_k\cdot s$,
$S_k \cap T_k = \emptyset$, and $|T_k| = |S_k| \ge \kappa$
($\kappa = 3$). A set $X$ of valid links is feasible when it satisfies
\begin{description}
\item[(F1)] at most one link per name, and
\item[(F2)] $S_k \cap T_{k'} = \emptyset$ for $k \ne k'$
  (equivalently: every source rank of a chosen link is kept).
\end{description}
Let $\Links$ be the full universe (\texttt{enumerate\_all\_links\_} with
sub-runs), and write $x_r \in \{0,1\}$ for ``rank $r$ is kept in $C$''
and $y_k \in \{0,1\}$ for ``link $k$ is accepted''. The model
\texttt{ilp\_baseline} writes is
\begin{align}
  \min\quad
    &\sum_{r=0}^{m-1} x_r
    \label{eq:obj} \\
  \text{s.t.}\quad
    &\sum_{k:\,c_k=c} y_k \;\le\; 1
      && \forall\, c \in \Names
      \tag{F1} \label{eq:F1} \\
    &x_r + y_k \;\le\; 1
      && \forall\, k \in \Links,\; r \in T_k
      \tag{drop} \label{eq:drop} \\
    &x_r \;\ge\; y_k
      && \forall\, k \in \Links,\; r \in S_k
      \tag{pin} \label{eq:pin} \\
    &x_r + \sum_{k:\,r \in T_k} y_k \;\ge\; 1
      && \forall\, r
      \tag{keep} \label{eq:keep} \\
    &x_r, y_k \;\in\; \{0,1\}.
      \nonumber
\end{align}
\eqref{eq:drop} and \eqref{eq:pin} together are (F2) plus the
self-disjointness $S_k \cap T_k = \emptyset$ already required of a valid
link. \eqref{eq:keep} forbids dropping a rank that no accepted link
covers. The objective is exactly $|C|$.

This is the right model. Its size is not: $|\Links|$ is the number of
clash-free sub-runs, which for a maximal source run of length $L$ is
$\Theta(L^2)$. Summing over runs of total length $O(m)$ per shift gives
up to $\Theta(a_{\max} m^2)$ binaries $y_k$ and as many
\eqref{eq:drop}/\eqref{eq:pin} rows. On
\texttt{repeat\_100pct\_x200\_y50} with contiguous $k{=}21$ and
$a_{\max}{=}8$ that is already $\approx 7.7{\times}10^6$ sub-runs; the
LP cannot be written.

\section{Maximal source runs}
\label{sec:runs}

Fix a shift $a \in \{1,\dots,a_{\max}\}$. For each gapped-SA rank $r$ let
$\tau_a(r)$ be the rank whose lextext position is the $a$-successor of
$r$ (\texttt{succ\_lexpos\_}), or undefined if that successor does not
exist. Scan the ranks in order and group consecutive $r$ whose successor
exists and carries a common target name. Each group
$[ \alpha, \beta )$ of length $L = \beta - \alpha \ge \kappa$ is a
\textbf{maximal source run}
\[
  R \;=\; \bigl( a,\; [\alpha,\beta),\; \tau_a,\; c \bigr),
\]
where $c = \name(\tau_a(\alpha))$ is the target. Write $\Runs$ for the
set of all such runs over $a = 1,\dots,a_{\max}$. There are
$O(m a_{\max})$ of them, and they partition, per $a$, the ranks that
have an $a$-successor.

Every link that \texttt{enumerate\_all\_links\_} emits is a sub-interval
$[\alpha+p, \alpha+q] \subseteq [\alpha,\beta)$ of some $R \in \Runs$
with length at least $\kappa$ and with no \emph{clash}: no source rank
in the sub-interval has its successor also in the sub-interval (that is
exactly $S \cap T = \emptyset$). Conversely every clash-free sub-interval
of a maximal run is a valid link in $\Links$. So $\Links$ is the set of
clash-free sub-intervals of members of $\Runs$, and it is enough to
choose, per run, at most one such sub-interval.

\section{The compact formulation}
\label{sec:compact}

For each run $R = (a,[\alpha,\beta), \tau, c)$ of length $L$ introduce
binaries $w_{R,t}$ and $\sigma_{R,t}$ for $t = 0,\dots,L-1$:
\begin{align*}
  w_{R,t} &= 1 &&\text{iff source rank $\alpha+t$ lies in the selected
                 sub-run of $R$,} \\
  \sigma_{R,t} &= 1 &&\text{iff that sub-run \emph{starts} at $t$.}
\end{align*}
The selected set $\{ t : w_{R,t} = 1 \}$ is forced to be a (possibly
empty) interval by
\begin{align}
  \sigma_{R,0} &= w_{R,0},
    \label{eq:st0} \\
  \sigma_{R,t} &= w_{R,t} \land \lnot w_{R,t-1}
    && t = 1,\dots,L-1,
    \label{eq:st} \\
  w_{R,t} &\ge w_{R,t-1} + w_{R,t+1} - 1
    && t = 1,\dots,L-2,
    \label{eq:hole} \\
  \sum_{t=0}^{L-1} \sigma_{R,t} &\le 1,
    \label{eq:onestart} \\
  \sum_{t=0}^{L-1} w_{R,t}
    &\ge \kappa \sum_{t=0}^{L-1} \sigma_{R,t}.
    \label{eq:len}
\end{align}
\eqref{eq:st} linearizes as the three inequalities
$\sigma \le w_t$, $\sigma \le 1 - w_{t-1}$,
$\sigma \ge w_t - w_{t-1}$. \eqref{eq:hole} forbids a hole in a
consecutive block; together with at most one start, the occupied ranks
form a single interval. \eqref{eq:len} is $\kappa$ whenever the run is
used and $0$ otherwise.

One link per name is one selected start among all runs targeting $c$:
\begin{equation}
  \sum_{\substack{R \in \Runs \\ \mathrm{target}(R)=c}}
    \sum_{t} \sigma_{R,t}
  \;\le\; 1
  \qquad \forall\, c \in \Names.
  \tag{F1$'$} \label{eq:F1c}
\end{equation}
Pin, drop, self-clash, and keep become, for each $R$ and each offset
$t$ with successor $\tau(\alpha+t)$ defined,
\begin{align}
  x_{\alpha+t} &\ge w_{R,t},
    \tag{pin$'$} \label{eq:pinc} \\
  x_{\tau(\alpha+t)} + w_{R,t} &\le 1,
    \tag{drop$'$} \label{eq:dropc} \\
  w_{R,t} + w_{R,\, \tau(\alpha+t)-\alpha} &\le 1
    && \text{if } \tau(\alpha+t) \in [\alpha,\beta),
    \tag{clash} \label{eq:clash} \\
  x_r + \sum_{\substack{R,t \\ \tau_R(\alpha_R+t)=r}} w_{R,t}
    &\ge 1
    && \forall\, r.
    \tag{keep$'$} \label{eq:keepc}
\end{align}
The objective is still \eqref{eq:obj}. All variables are binary.

\begin{proposition}[Equivalence]
\label{prop:equiv}
The compact ILP and the expanded ILP \eqref{eq:obj}--\eqref{eq:keep}
have the same optimal value. There is a bijection between feasible
$y$-supports of the expanded model and feasible $(w,\sigma)$-supports of
the compact model that preserves the kept-set $(x_r)$.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}[Proof sketch]
Given a feasible $y$, for each name at most one $k$ has $y_k=1$
by \eqref{eq:F1}. That $k$ is a clash-free sub-interval of a unique
maximal run $R$; set $w_{R,t}=1$ on that sub-interval, $\sigma$ at its
start, and all other $w,\sigma$ of runs targeting the same name to $0$.
\eqref{eq:st0}--\eqref{eq:len} hold by construction,
\eqref{eq:F1c} is \eqref{eq:F1}, \eqref{eq:pinc}--\eqref{eq:keepc} are
\eqref{eq:pin}--\eqref{eq:keep} rewritten in occupancy variables, and
\eqref{eq:clash} is $S_k \cap T_k = \emptyset$ on a consecutive block.
Conversely, a feasible $(w,\sigma)$ of a run $R$ is either all-zero or a
single interval of length $\ge \kappa$. \eqref{eq:clash} makes that
interval clash-free, hence a valid link $k \in \Links$; set $y_k=1$.
\eqref{eq:F1c} gives \eqref{eq:F1}. The two $x$-vectors coincide, so the
objectives coincide.
\end{proof}

Restricting to \emph{maximal} links
(\texttt{--universe maximal}) is the compact model plus
$\sum_t w_{R,t} = L \sum_t \sigma_{R,t}$ for every $R$: if a run is used,
the whole run is used. That is a restriction of $\Links$, so its optimum
is an upper bound on true $|C|^\star$, not a lower bound.
\texttt{--universe legacy} is a further restriction (the heuristics'
\texttt{enumerate\_candidates\_} view) and is not a lower bound either.

\section{Size}
\label{sec:size}

Each rank belongs to at most one run per shift, so there are at most
$m a_{\max}$ occupancy bits $w_{R,t}$ and as many starts $\sigma_{R,t}$,
plus $m$ keep-bits $x_r$. Constraints are $O(1)$ per occupancy bit
except \eqref{eq:keepc}, which has $O(a_{\max})$ terms per rank. The
whole model is $O(m a_{\max})$ binaries and $O(m a_{\max})$ rows,
against $O(\sum_R L_R^2)$ binaries for the expanded form.

On the $200\times 50$ tandem with $k{=}21$ and $a_{\max}{=}8$:

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}lrrrr@{}}
\toprule
model & $|\Runs|$ / $|\Links|$ & binaries & rows & notes \\
\midrule
expanded, full
  & ${\sim}7.7{\times}10^6$ sub-runs
  & ${\sim}7.7{\times}10^6$
  & does not write
  & $\Theta(L^2)$ per run of length ${\sim}200$ \\
compact, full
  & $400$ runs
  & ${\sim}1.7{\times}10^5$
  & ${\sim}4.8{\times}10^5$
  & equivalent to the row above \\
expanded, maximal
  & $400$ links
  & $10\,401$
  & $168\,219$
  & restriction; opt $|C|=1285$ \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

The maximal-universe expanded model is what
\texttt{ilp\_baseline --universe maximal} currently emits; CBC solves it
in tens of seconds on this instance (heuristics sit at $1473$, except
greedy-size at $1670$). The compact full model is the one that matches
\texttt{--universe full} without materializing the sub-runs.

\section{A name-level relaxation}
\label{sec:name}

A coarser, still valid, lower bound drops copy-index alignment. Let
$n_c = |I_c|$ for names with $n_c \ge \kappa$, and let $F_0$ be the
number of ranks in intervals smaller than $\kappa$ (those ranks are
never dropped). For each run $R$ targeting $c$ from a source name $s$
of length $L_R$, a binary $y_R$ and an integer coverage $c_c \in [0,n_c]$
satisfy
\begin{align}
  \sum_{R:\,\mathrm{target}(R)=c} y_R &\le 1, \\
  c_c &\le \sum_{R:\,\mathrm{target}(R)=c} L_R\, y_R, \\
  c_c &\ge \kappa \sum_{R:\,\mathrm{target}(R)=c} y_R, \\
  y_R = 1 &\implies c_c + c_s \le n_s.
\end{align}
The last implication is source integrity at name granularity: dropping
$c_c$ ranks of $c$ using $s$ requires $c_c$ kept ranks of $s$. Several
targets may share the same source; each is limited by $n_s - c_s$
independently, which is correct for free source sharing. Then
\[
  |C|^\star \;\ge\; F_0 + \sum_c (n_c - c_c).
\]
This ignores that a sub-run occupies specific copy indices, so it
underestimates $|C|$ whenever two names need overlapping, not nested,
kept slots of a common source. It is cheap ($O(|\Runs|)$ binaries) and
strictly tighter than summing per-name best coverage, which treats
source sharing as unbounded.

\section{Where this sits}
\label{sec:where}

\texttt{ilp\_baseline} should keep writing the expanded model on small
texts --- the $y_k$ are easy to decode-check, and the ten-case suite is
tiny. On repetitive inputs the expanded full universe does not fit;
the compact model of \S\ref{sec:compact} is the same integer program
and is what a full-universe solve on those inputs has to use.
\texttt{--universe maximal} remains a fast restriction, useful as an
upper bound on $|C|^\star$ and as a check that the compact encoding of
maximal runs matches the expanded $400$-link LP.
